Inventory Intelligence

Inventory planning, anomaly detection and local AI-assisted analysis.

PLANNING ENGINE

Inventory Planning

DreiTrack analyses recorded inventory activity to estimate stock coverage and identify items that may require procurement attention.

Planning calculations use stock movements, available inventory, outstanding purchase orders, minimum stock levels and supplier lead times.

DETECTION ENGINE

Anomaly Detection

DreiTrack reviews inventory activity for unusual usage, repeated stock adjustments, supplier lead-time changes and overdue purchase orders.

Flags indicate activity that may require review. They do not automatically mean an error, inventory loss or misconduct has occurred.
